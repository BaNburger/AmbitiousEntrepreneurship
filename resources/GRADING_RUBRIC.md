# Grading & The Revenue Ladder

Updated: 2026-10-26.

Published in week 0. No surprises: this is exactly how your semester is evaluated.

## The One Principle

**Evidence quality and decision discipline weigh as much as revenue.** This course grades how well you ran the experiment called "your startup" — not just how much money came out of it. A rigorously argued kill with a documented evidence pack can earn an A. A zombie project limping to demo day cannot.

## The Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | What an A looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence quality** | 30% | Interview logs with named contacts and verbatim quotes · pre-committed thresholds in the Kill Criteria Log · UTM-verified traffic · honest small-n read-outs. Your Toolkit tells a complete, auditable story. |
| **Decision discipline** | 30% | You wrote pass/fail lines BEFORE tests and honored them · Go/Pivot/Kill decisions argued from data, not attachment · pivots and kills documented with what the evidence said. |
| **Verified traction** | 30% | Progress along the Revenue Ladder (below) · revenue quality (repeatable > consulting) · outside-social-graph, dashboard-verified. |
| **Communication** | 10% | Demo-day pitch: clear problem evidence, honest numbers, a story that survives Q&A. |

## The A-Kill (yes, really)

A team that: ran 25+ real interviews, launched a landing page against a pre-committed threshold, built and shipped a prototype, ran the riskiest-assumption test, and then **killed the idea in Module 07 with a documented autopsy** — then restarted via the concierge ramp and sold ANYTHING by demo day — has demonstrated more entrepreneurial skill than a team that limped to €800 on favors. Grade accordingly: that's an A trajectory.

What an A-kill requires: the full evidence pack, the pre-committed kill criterion it violated, the Kill Ritual one-pager, and a demo-day presentation of what was learned and what came next.

## The Revenue Ladder

| Week | Pass bar | Stretch (leaderboard) |
|---|---|---|
| Module 08 | First € from outside your social graph | 3 paying users |
| Module 09 | €500 cumulative | €1,000 |
| Module 10 | €1,000 | €3,000 |
| Module 11 | €2,000 | €5,000 |
| **Demo Day** | **€2,000 verified minimum + evidence pack** | **The €10,000 club** |

**What counts:** revenue from outside your social graph only, meaning people you did not know before this venture (no friends, family, classmates, course teams) · any amount, a paid deposit or pre-order too · verified via payment dashboard or a paid numbered invoice · repeatable revenue (same offer, >1 customer, no custom scope) ranks above consulting-style projects · "paid twice" beats "paid once." A signed pilot or letter of intent without payment is signal, not revenue.

**What doesn't count:** favor purchases, team-to-team purchases inside the course, unverifiable cash, revenue you can't show a dashboard or invoice for.

## Anti-Fake Rules (published, so nobody is surprised)

Interviews require named contacts + one verbatim quote each in the Toolkit; random 60-second recording spot-checks happen in check-ins. Landing-page traffic requires the UTM source report; only target-segment conversions count. To read a landing-page line, compare it with Unbounce's 2024 medians for SaaS landing pages, 16.9% for email traffic (warm), 4.1% for paid search and 2.9% for paid social (cold), where a conversion is any click or form event ([Unbounce, 2024](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/saas-conversion-rate/)). AI-simulated users are allowed for PREP only, never as evidence, and must be labeled.
