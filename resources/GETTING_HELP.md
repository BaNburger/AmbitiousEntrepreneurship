# Getting help

Updated: 2026-10-26.

A hard week is part of founding. When it is more than that, talk to someone outside your team. It is a strength, not a failure.

## Talk to someone

- **Your doctor.** A good first stop for sleep, exhaustion or anything that worries you.
- **Your university's counselling service.** Free and confidential for students. Your course run names the one at your host.
- **A founder you trust,** a mentor or a peer group. Someone who has been there.
