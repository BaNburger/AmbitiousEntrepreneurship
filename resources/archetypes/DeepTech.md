# Deep Tech — when the product is still in the lab

Updated: 2026-10-20.

**What changes:** your riskiest assumption may be technical — but the commercial clock still runs. You validate demand for the OUTCOME before the tech is ready, exactly like Dropbox validated with a video.

**Your wallet-equivalents (honest hierarchy):** paid feasibility study/pilot from an industrial partner (best — real money) > LOI with concrete numbers and conditions (good — conditional wallet) > EXIST/grant awarded (money, but a jury is not a customer — label it) > "strategic interest" emails (signal only). The revenue bars: one €3k paid feasibility study can clear multiple weekly bars — deal size compensates count.

**Your ladder translation:** M04 landing page: a technical one-pager + outcome demo video sent to 20 named industry contacts, threshold on meeting-requests. M06 prototype: simulation/benchmark that proves the CLAIM: build the smallest thing that tests the physics. M07 monkey-first is your module — name the assumption that kills it. M08: a paid feasibility study counts. An LOI with numbers is signal until money arrives (framework 03, question 7). M09: a fixed-scope feasibility study at a list price, ordered through a form that issues the invoice. M10 retention: a paid pilot that turns into a follow-on order is your "paid again". Agree its success criteria before the pilot (Module 09). M12: your funding path skews grants/strategic (Celonis started on EXIST).

**Your traps:** "we'll validate after the paper", grant-chasing as revenue substitute (two grants  is not  PMF), pilots that never convert because success criteria were never agreed (agree success criteria before the pilot, Module 09).

**Kill criteria that bite:** no industrial partner will pay even a token feasibility fee after the number of asks you set before the test · the technical monkey fails its own benchmark twice · every "customer" wants it free until it's perfect.
