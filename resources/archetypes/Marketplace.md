# Marketplace — two customers, one chicken, one egg

Updated: 2026-10-20.

**What changes:** you validate TWO segments, and sequence matters. Rule of thumb: nail supply first (they feel the pain of empty calendars/shelves), then buy demand with concentration — one city, one category, one week.

**Your ladder translation:** M03 pain maps: one per side; the hair-on-fire side becomes your wedge. M04: two landing pages OR one supply-side page (supply commitments are behavior). M06 prototype: a spreadsheet + WhatsApp concierge marketplace is a REAL prototype (do things that don't scale — Airbnb, bonus case). M08 first €: your first take-rate euro on a manually-matched transaction counts fully. M09: let your payment provider's marketplace product split each payment. Only your cut counts. M10 retention: count return on both sides. Supply that leaves after one transaction is your first churn. Revenue bars: GMV is vanity — only YOUR cut counts on the leaderboard.

**Your traps:** building matching algorithms before manually matching 20 transactions, spreading across cities, subsidizing both sides and calling it traction.

**Kill criteria that bite:** supply churns after the first transaction · you can't concentrate liquidity in ONE niche by a date you set before the test · your take rate drops to €0 whenever you stop pushing by hand. Each line gets its number and date before the test (Module 07).
