# B2C — when your customer is a person, not a company

**What changes:** volume replaces depth. You can't interview a "segment lead" — you need many small signals and fast funnels. Prices are small, so the revenue bars are met through COUNT, not deal size.

**Your ladder translation:** M03 interviews → 10+ short intercepts (Mensa queue counts) instead of 5 deep ones, same verbatim-quote rule. M04 landing page → conversion matters MORE for you; threshold on signup→waitlist. M08 first € → first 10 paying users beats one €300 pilot; a €4.99 payment IS a wallet opening. M09 pricing → subscriptions & one-time unlocks; test annual vs monthly early. Revenue bars: hit them via user count (€1,000 = ~200 × €4.99 — that's a real distribution test, which is exactly the point).

**Your traps:** friends-and-family installs (social-graph rule applies hard), app-store review theater instead of retention, building for "everyone 18-35". Superhuman (M09 case) is your pricing case: consumer product, premium price, single plan.

**Kill criteria that bite:** D7 retention < 10% after two honest cohorts · CAC > 3× first-purchase value with no organic pull · nobody shares it unprompted after 100 users.
