# B2C — when your customer is a person, not a company

Updated: 2026-10-20.

**What changes:** volume replaces depth. You can't interview a "segment lead" — you need many small signals and fast funnels. Prices are small, so the revenue bars are met through COUNT, not deal size.

**Your ladder translation:** M03 interviews: 10+ short intercepts (Mensa queue counts) instead of 5 deep ones, same verbatim-quote rule. M04 landing page: conversion matters MORE for you; threshold on signup to waitlist. M08 first €: a €4.99 payment IS a wallet opening. Small prices need more asks. M09 pricing: subscriptions & one-time unlocks; test annual vs monthly early. M10 retention: count week-one return across two cohorts from your product's records. App-store reviews are not retention. Revenue bars: hit them via user count (€1,000 = ~200 × €4.99 — that's a real distribution test, which is exactly the point).

**Your traps:** friends-and-family installs (social-graph rule applies hard), app-store review theater instead of retention, building for "everyone 18-35".

**Kill criteria that bite:** too few users come back in week one, across two honest cohorts · acquiring a customer costs more than the first purchase brings, with no organic pull · nobody shares it unprompted. Each line gets its number and date before the test (Module 07).
