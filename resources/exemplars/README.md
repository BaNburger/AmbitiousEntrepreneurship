# Exemplars — what "good" actually looks like

Five synthetic-but-realistic artifacts at the quality bar the rubric rewards. **These are invented teaching examples** (labeled on every page) — two fictional teams, numbers made internally consistent. Your artifacts will look messier. That's fine: the bar is **specificity, not polish**. Named humans, verbatim quotes, committed numbers, honest read-outs.
