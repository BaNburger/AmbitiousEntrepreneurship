# Exemplar: Pain Map — Team TurbineTrack (M03)

*Synthetic exemplar — realistic but invented. Note what makes it good: named humans, receipts not adjectives, and the budget column filled in.*

**Problem hypothesis:** Independent wind-turbine service crews (3–20 technicians) lose billable hours because maintenance findings are captured on paper/WhatsApp and retyped into reports days later.

**Verbatim gold (the quotes that decided it):**
- "Every Friday I sit two hours matching photos to turbine IDs. I would pay real money to never do that again." — Markus B., 14.03.
- "We lost a €9k claim because the finding wasn't documented the same day." — Jens K., 17.03.
- "If it doesn't work offline at 90 meters in the wind, it doesn't exist." — Markus B. (constraint, went straight into M05 scoping)

**Hair-on-fire verdict:** Independent SMEs, owner-led, 5–20 techs. Daily frequency + money already spent on workarounds + owner IS the budget. In-house crews fail the budget-authority test (procurement), freelancers fail intensity (externalized pain).
