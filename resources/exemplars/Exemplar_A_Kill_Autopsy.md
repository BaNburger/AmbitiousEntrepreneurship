# Exemplar: The A-Grade Kill — Team Klausurly (M07 to Demo Day)

Updated: 2026-10-20.

*Synthetic exemplar — realistic but invented. This is the artifact the rubric means by "a rigorously argued kill can be an A." Read it even if you never kill.*

## One-page autopsy, the Kill Ritual (week 8)

**What we believed (Module 02).** Students will pay for AI-generated practice exams in their professor's exact style.

### 1. What the evidence said

We tested in order. 14 Mom Test interviews (Module 03), then a landing page against a line of 10% of segment visitors (the course default since Module 04), which got 13.4% (Module 04). Then click dummies (Module 05) and a live prototype with 41 users and strong exam-week usage (Module 06). Then **the wallet. €4.99 per exam, and 0 of 9 paid** (Module 07 week). Our B2B pivot test asked 10 named tutoring schools in 5 days and got 2 replies and no interest ("we ARE the content").

Cause of death is free-alternative gravity. The pain is real, but free substitutes absorb all of it (forums, Moodle, ChatGPT).

### 2. Which line it crossed

"At least 3 buyers in the first 10 live asks", written in our Kill Criteria Log before the first ask. We got 0 of 9. The B2B test had its own line, written before the first school was asked, and missed it too. **KILLED 16.05, announced in check-in.**

### 3. What surprised us

Usage-love is not willingness to pay. Our own glossary called it, 41 fans and 0 customers. And the Fachschaft channel that brought our visitors (12.9% CTR) was the strongest asset we had, stronger than the product.

### 4. What we'd tell the next founder

Ask "have you ever paid anything for this?" in week 2. We had the answer, 2 of 14, in our own Interview Log and didn't weight it. It cost us 6 weeks.

## The Restart Ramp (same week)

Kill Ritual done (this page). 48h re-ideation from OUR artifacts, starting from the surprise, the Fachschaft channel. **Concierge restart:** "Klausur-Countdown", a paid, human-curated 4-week exam-prep sprint for Statik (the exam with the loudest panic quotes), €29, capped at 30 seats, sold through the same channel.

## Outcome by Demo Day

23 seats sold × €29 = **€667 verified** (Stripe dashboard, zero social-graph buyers — Fachschaft channel is outside the rubric's exclusion) · 19 of 23 completed · 11 said they'd buy the next one · second cohort waitlist: 31.

## Why this is an A trajectory (grading rationale)

Evidence quality: every claim above cites an artifact (logs, thresholds, dashboards). Decision discipline: two pre-committed thresholds honored AGAINST the team's attachment; kill announced publicly; no zombie weeks. Verified traction: €667 real revenue post-restart — less money than the top teams, more decision quality than almost anyone. Communication: this page IS the demo-day story.

*The lesson to steal: the kill wasn't the end of the company — it was the moment the team stopped spending weeks on a dead hypothesis and started charging for a live one.*
