## Legal & Fundraising

### YC SAFE Agreement (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)
https://www.ycombinator.com/documents
The standard early-stage investment document. Used by thousands of startups. Free, open-source. Don't let a lawyer charge you €5,000 to draft something worse.

### Sequoia Capital — Writing a Business Plan
https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan/
The industry-standard pitch deck structure. Problem → Solution → Why Now → Market → Competition → Product → Business Model → Team → Financials → Ask.

## Building & Prototyping

### Vercel + v0.dev
https://v0.dev
Generate full frontend UIs from text descriptions. Best for landing pages and MVPs.

### Replit
https://replit.com
Build and deploy web apps from your browser. Free tier is enough for prototypes.

### Claude / ChatGPT
For everything else — writing copy, analyzing interviews, generating code, brainstorming solutions. Use AI as your first employee.

## Validation & PMF Measurement

### Sean Ellis PMF Survey (Free Tool)
https://pmfsurvey.com
Ready-made survey to measure product-market fit. Send to users, get your PMF score. The 40% "very disappointed" benchmark. No setup required.

### Sequoia Arc PMF Framework (Free)
https://sequoiacap.com/article/pmf-framework/
Sequoia's internal framework, now publicly available. Three archetypes of PMF. The best mental model for understanding where you are.

## Learning (Beyond the Reading List)

### YC Startup School (Free)
https://www.startupschool.org
7-week free curriculum + 20+ hours of lectures. Pick the ones relevant to your current module.

### TechStars Entrepreneur's Toolkit (Free)
https://toolkit.techstars.com
Modules on Lean Canvas, pitch, PMF (W3 framework), and founder mental health. Concise and practical.

### First Round Review
https://review.firstround.com
The best long-form articles on startup building. Searchable by topic. Start with their "Must Reads" collection.

### a16z AI Canon
https://a16z.com/ai-canon/
Curated list of the most important AI papers, courses, and guides. Essential for AI-first founders.

### Indie Hackers
https://www.indiehackers.com
Real founders sharing real revenue numbers, strategies, and struggles. Good for staying grounded.
