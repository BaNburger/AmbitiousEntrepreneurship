# Workshop 07 — Kill Your Darlings

> Module 07 · The Kill Decision · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 15m | **Pre-Mortem** | Imagine it’s 6 months later and you failed. WHY? |
| 15m | **Kill Criteria** | Define 3 things that would make you STOP right now |
| 20m | **GPK Scoring** | Score all 9 questions of the Go/Pivot/Kill framework |
| 20m | **Peer Tribunal** | Other teams challenge your evidence. Defend or fold. |
| 20m | **The Decision** | Go, Pivot, or Kill. Announce it publicly. No hedging. |

## Discussion Sparks

- “Celebrate kills. The fastest way to success is killing zombies.”
- “What evidence would change your mind? If nothing—you’re in denial.”
- “Would you invest YOUR money in this? Be honest.”
