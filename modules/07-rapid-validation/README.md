# Module 07 — The Kill Decision

Updated: 2026-10-30.

> **Core question:** Why should this startup die?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after 4 to 6 hours of preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M07-LG1.** Students can run a pre-mortem on their venture as it stands, check the riskiest assumptions they logged from Module 00 on against seven weeks of evidence, and write every cause of death that still stands as an assumption about the customer, the solution or the business.
2. **M07-LG2.** Students can sort those assumptions by how critical they are and how much their evidence already shows, pick the monkey, the critical unknown that kills the venture if it is false, instead of a pedestal that feels like progress, and test it this week with the cheapest experiment that could prove it wrong, against a line written before the first result.
3. **M07-LG3.** Students can decide Go, Pivot or Kill for their venture on criteria committed in advance, honour every line in their Kill Criteria Log, score the rest on artifacts rather than opinions, name a pivot as type 1 (another solution) or type 2 (back to the problem space), and announce the verdict, provisional until the monkey test has run.
4. **M07-LG4.** Students can kill well, writing down what the evidence said, which committed line it crossed and what they would tell the next founder, and, when the venture dies, restart from their own records, the problems, segments and channels seven weeks have produced, with a concierge offer they can sell in Module 08.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 08, your team has tested the riskiest assumption left in your venture, decided Go, Pivot or Kill on criteria committed in advance, and holds the offer you will sell next, even after a kill.

**This week's bar:** Test your monkey with 10 people per student from your segment against a line logged first, decide Go, Pivot or Kill on your Kill Criteria Log and scorecard, and bring the verdict and your next offer to the Module 08 check-in. The learning floor is the scored verdict and the offer per team and 5 such people per student. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
