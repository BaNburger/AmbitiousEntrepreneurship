# Module 01 — Team & Foundations

> **Core question:** Why do the best teams beat the best ideas?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M00 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Ideas are cheap, execution compounds — why investors bet on teams
2. Co-founder fit: complementary skills, shared conviction, aligned ambition
3. Feedback culture from day one: radical candor and the 4A framework
4. Startup ≠ small business: growth is a deliberate choice

**This week's bar:** team formed + 10 logged conversations + 3 ranked problem hypotheses. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
