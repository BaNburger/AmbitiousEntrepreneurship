# Workshop 01 — Problem Safari

> Module 01 · Team & Foundations · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 10m | **Safari Briefing** | Rules: talk to 3+ strangers about frustrations, not ideas |
| 30m | **Field Work** | LEAVE THE CLASSROOM. Talk to real humans on campus. (Rain plan: cafeteria, library, Mensa queue — anywhere with strangers) |
| 20m | **Pattern Mining** | Share findings, cluster problems, find overlaps |
| 20m | **Team Formation** | Form teams around shared problem passion |
| 10m | **Problem Commit** | Each team defines top 3 problem hypotheses |

## Discussion Sparks

- “Did anyone find a problem they’d personally pay €50/month to solve?”
- “What surprised you most about what strangers complained about?”
- “Why is it so hard to leave the building and talk to people?”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Team formed + 3 problem hypotheses ranked by conviction

**Kill criteria:** Your team formed around a ‘cool technology’ instead of a real problem
