BehaviourOnly what you saw

you showed numbers on slide 10 that didn't match our model.

Homework · how to do it

Two jobs until next time, the team and the talks

THE TEAM

USE THE WEEK AS YOUR TRIAL

Fit shows under load, not over coffee

  • Answer the full Co-Founder Matchmaking Assessment alone, then talk through every gap
  • Hold at least two talks with a co-founder listening, then swap
  • Two builders and nobody selling? Give selling an owner by your midweek day, or recruit one
  • Hold your feedback round at the end of the week
  • Then update and confirm the agreement

Output the confirmed agreement

THE TALKS

FIND YOUR PEOPLE, STAY IN THE PROBLEM

Ten conversations per student in your shared problem space

  • Start with Kickoff (M00) contacts and their intros, people you see daily and where the work happens
  • Review sites and AI deep research show where they complain
  • Ask about the last time it happened and what it cost. Don't pitch. Log every problem, unranked
  • Record with the consent sentence in Legal Basics
  • Get a blind second opinion. Give an AI your raw notes, minus personal data and your favourite idea

Output talks in the Interview Log
