# Workshop 11: The Energy Audit

Updated: 2026-10-26.

> Module 11 · Founder Health · Workshop · [Goals M11-LG2 to LG4](README.md)

![Stress map](media/stressor-map.png "Map your sources in step 1.")

By the start of Module 12, each of you knows what drains you and what gives you energy, and your team has said how everyone is really doing, agreed on a pace it can hold and kept selling. Today starts all three, because founders who stay standing keep selling. Examples are made up.

**Today is personal.** Nothing you write is collected or read out. Share what you choose, sit out any step. [Where to get help](M11_Homework.md#where-to-get-help). **Bring** your [Student Toolkit](../../resources/Student_Toolkit.xlsx) and a pen.

## The 90 minutes

| Time | Step | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 25′ | 1 · Map what drains you | Map, stop line, practice |
| 40′ | 2 · Say how you really are | One change in the Team sheet |
| 25′ | 3 · Pack the scary bits first | Your week, team line, bar, asks sent |

### Step 1 · Map what drains you (25′)

**Goal.** What drains and energises you, for you only.

1. **Answer three questions (6′)** in writing. The last time I failed at something I cared about, what did I tell myself? When things go wrong, do I hide, push harder, blame, analyse or talk? If my startup failed next year, what would still be me?
2. **Map the last two weeks (8′).** Each stressful moment and its source. Use the six sources, or your own word.
3. **Map what gave you energy (5′).**
4. **Write your stop line (4′),** a sign others could notice, how often, and what you then do.
5. **Pick one practice (2′).** Sleep, exercise, an evening note, gratitude.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| Late payers drain me. "If I go silent twice this week, I tell my co-founder." | "Everything is stressful." |

**Output.** Map, stop line and practice, kept by you.

### Step 2 · Say how you really are (40′)

**Goal.** Every founder says how they are, and the team agrees one change.

1. **Open your Module 01 agreement (3′)** in the Team sheet.
2. **Go round once (12′).** Each founder says how they really are, plus one thing from their map. Listen, don't fix. Anyone may pass.
3. **Talk through five things (15′).** Needs, values, expectations, communication style, energy. What changed?
4. **Agree one change (7′)** until your next big deadline. Add an Agreement row "How we look after each other", with a revisit date.
5. **Confirm it (3′),** dated.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| "I hate cold calls." "Money keeps me up." Sunday off, revisit in four weeks. | "All good?" "All good." |

**Output.** One change in your Team sheet, every founder's yes.

### Step 3 · Pack the scary bits first (25′)

**Goal.** A week your team can hold that still sells.

1. **Name the scary bits (6′),** what must happen next and keeps slipping.
2. **Put them in first (6′),** each with an owner and a day.
3. **Write your team line (3′)** in that row, or take ours. "If one of us says the pace cannot be held, we cut next week's plan before we add work."
4. **Commit to the bar (5′).** "Reach €5,000 cumulative revenue from outside your social graph, keep the private maps you chose, of what drains and energises you, for seven days, and hold one honest co-founder conversation that ends in one agreed change in your Team sheet, with a revisit date. The learning floor is €2,000 cumulative and the conversation with its written change." Pick floor or full bar, and the euros by your midweek day.
5. **Sell before you leave (5′),** one follow-up each to a lead from your Pipeline or a customer's referral.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| Renewal calls Tuesday, second feature cut, two asks sent. | "Sales after Demo Day." |

**Output.** Your week, the team line, the bar picked, asks sent.
