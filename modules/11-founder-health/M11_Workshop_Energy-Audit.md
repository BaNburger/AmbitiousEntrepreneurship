# Workshop 11 — The Energy Audit

> Module 11 · Founder Health · 105 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 15m | **Honest Check-In** | How is your team REALLY doing? Rate 1-10 energy. |
| 20m | **Energy Mapping** | Map all 4 dimensions: physical, emotional, mental, spiritual |
| 20m | **Drain + Source ID** | Biggest energy drain? Biggest energy source? Per person. |
| 20m | **Protocol Design** | Create your personal non-negotiable well-being protocol |
| 15m | **Peer Support** | Share one thing you’re struggling with. No fixing, just listening. |
| 15m | **Revenue & Pipeline Pulse** | The sprint to €2,000 doesn’t pause: quick leaderboard + one unblock per team |

## Discussion Sparks

- “This isn’t therapy—it’s performance optimization for your most critical asset.”
- “72% of founders report mental health challenges. You are not alone.”
- “What would you do differently if you had to run this startup for 10 years?”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Personal well-being protocol + team health assessment

**Kill criteria:** Your team is burning out and nobody’s talking about it
