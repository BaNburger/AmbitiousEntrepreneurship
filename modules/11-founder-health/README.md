# Module 11 — Founder Health

Updated: 2026-10-30.

> **Core question:** How do you build a company without breaking yourself?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after 4 to 6 hours of preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M11-LG1.** Students can plan for failure without fearing it, explaining why most startups fail, keeping the event ("the startup failed") apart from the identity ("I am a failure"), and, if it comes to that, closing a company in the right order, with investors told first, the legal duties of their company form met and nobody left with a grudge.
2. **M11-LG2.** Students can explain with research why founding is stressful even for founders who love it, naming its stressors (uncertainty, money, social pressure, workload, expectations and loneliness) and why failure hurts, and map for a week what stresses them and what gives them energy.
3. **M11-LG3.** Students can look after themselves as founders, building an identity that rests on more than the startup, choosing the few practices with evidence behind them that they will actually keep, such as sleep, exercise, reflection or gratitude, and knowing when to talk to someone outside the team or to a professional.
4. **M11-LG4.** Students can look after their co-founders, saying honestly how each of them is doing, revisiting their Module 01 agreement on needs, expectations, time and energy now that the work is under load, and agreeing on a pace the team can hold to Demo Day and beyond, the people first, then the product and the profits, while the selling goes on.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 12, each of you knows what drains you and what gives you energy, and your team has said how everyone is really doing, agreed on a pace it can hold and kept selling.

**This week's bar:** Reach €5,000 cumulative revenue from outside your social graph, keep the private maps you chose, of what drains and energises you, for seven days, and hold one honest co-founder conversation that ends in one agreed change in your Team sheet, with a revisit date. The learning floor is €2,000 cumulative and the conversation with its written change. Rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
