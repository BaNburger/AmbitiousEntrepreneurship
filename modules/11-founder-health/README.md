# Module 11 — Founder Health

> **Core question:** How do you build a company without breaking yourself?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M10 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Energy management beats time management
2. The four dimensions of energy: body, emotions, mind, purpose
3. Resilience is a trainable skill, not a personality trait
4. Sustainable pace: Eisenhower, 80/20 and real deep work

**Revenue bar this week:** pass €2,000 · stretch €5,000 — outside your social graph, verified. Full rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
