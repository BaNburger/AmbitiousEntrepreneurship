# Module 10 — Product Management & Growth

Updated: 2026-10-26.

> **Core question:** What do you build next — and what do you refuse to build?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M10-LG1.** Students can tell whether their product keeps the customers it wins, count retention per cohort of paying customers and activated users from what the product records, read churn as the limit to growth, and put Sean Ellis's question, how they would feel if they could no longer use the product, to people who used the core, reading the answers as a compass and retention as the verdict to find who would miss the product most.
2. **M10-LG2.** Students can choose one North Star metric that counts the value paying customers get, break it into a KPI tree of a few drivers from the funnel to retention, give each driver an owner, a baseline and the lever that moves it, and read the tree every week from their own records.
3. **M10-LG3.** Students can decide what to build next from evidence, write every request, idea and fix as a bet that names who asked, the driver it should move and the effect expected before it is built, keep only the bets a paying customer asked for and that deepen the core outcome, rank those by impact and effort, for example with RICE, keep a short Now, Next and Later list, and say no to the rest, to a paying customer too, without losing them.
4. **M10-LG4.** Students can run their product on a weekly rhythm without heavy process, choose from their tree whether the next stretch is for building or for growing, ship the week's bets and read each against its expected effect, hang the plan on an outside deadline the team cannot move, and keep one founder on the pipeline every week so selling never stops while they build.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 11, your team knows which customers your product keeps and why, has built only what they need to stay and pay again, and runs each week on one number your records feed.

**This week's bar:** Reach €3,000 cumulative revenue from outside your social graph, shown in your payment dashboard or on paid numbered invoices. The learning floor is €1,000 cumulative. Rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).

**The arc:** You arrive with paying customers, and this week you find out which of them stay. €10k by Demo Day is the ambition, €100k the road after.
