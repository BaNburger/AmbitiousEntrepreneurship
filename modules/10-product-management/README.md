# Module 10 — Product Management & Growth

> **Core question:** What do you build next — and what do you refuse to build?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M09 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Measuring product-market fit: the 40% test and leading indicators
2. AARRR and KPI trees: instrument what matters, ignore the rest
3. Ruthless prioritization: RICE and the 3-question filter
4. Build vs. grow: allocating your scarcest resource — founder time

**Revenue bar this week:** pass €1,000 · stretch €3,000 — outside your social graph, verified. Full rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.pdf).

**The arc:** from the first €10k toward €100k — repeatable, automated, boringly reliable. Lean stack (Make.com, not Snowflake), laser focus for 3–5 people.
