# Module 10 — Product Management & Growth

> **Core question:** What do you build next — and what do you refuse to build?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M09 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Refuse most features — the 3-question filter beats the backlog
2. One North Star, one KPI tree — every driver gets an owner
3. Prioritize like it's money: RICE + Now/Next/Later, hung on a heartbeat you don't control (Fadell — not OKRs)
4. Grow where it compounds: retention first; founder hours are the scarcest capital
5. Measure PMF honestly: Ellis as compass, retention as court — PMF is the V1 gate on Moore's chasm curve

**Revenue bar this week:** pass €1,000 · stretch €3,000 — outside your social graph, verified. Full rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).

**The arc:** from the first €10k toward €100k — repeatable, automated, boringly reliable. Lean stack (Make.com, not Snowflake), laser focus for 3–5 people.
