# Workshop 10 — Feature Graveyard

> Module 10 · Product Management & Growth · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 10m | **Revenue Leaderboard** | Numbers on the wall: revenue, pipeline, best objection of the week |
| 10m | **Backlog Dump** | List EVERY feature idea and request in your backlog |
| 20m | **3-Question Filter** | Asked for? 10x better? Build by hand? Kill the rest. |
| 15m | **Feature Funeral** | Publicly kill features. Celebrate the graveyard. |
| 20m | **PMF Survey** | Send the Sean Ellis survey to ALL real users right now |
| 15m | **Improvement Loop** | Read results honestly (n is small!) + plan next iteration |

## Discussion Sparks

- “If <40% say ‘very disappointed’ without your product, you have work to do.”
- “The best product decision is the feature you chose NOT to build.”
- “Dark mode is not a feature. It’s procrastination with CSS.”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Clean backlog + PMF score + improvement plan

**Kill criteria:** Your backlog has 20+ features and you can’t prioritize them
