## The 90 minutes

| Time | Step | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 25′ | 1 · Autopsy your own startup | Your riskiest untested assumption, logged |
| 25′ | 2 · Write hypotheses you can lose | 3 ranked hypotheses, their evidence, a kill line |
| 40′ | 3 · Reach real people, then commit | Messages sent, target, pace, possible teammates |

### Step 1 · Autopsy your own startup (25′)

**Goal.** Find the customer assumption most likely to kill your idea.

1. **Write your starting point** in one line.
2. **Kill it.** It's one year from now and your startup is dead. Write down why, alone, in 5 minutes.
3. **Turn each cause into a belief.** "We assumed [who] has [problem] and pays to fix it."
4. **Circle the one** that kills you if it's wrong and that nobody has tested.
5. **Defend it in pairs.** Your partner asks "How do you know?" until you hit an opinion.
6. **Log it** in the Toolkit's Weekly Reflection.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| "We assumed lab managers in biotech start-ups lose hours on reagent orders and would pay to stop." | "We assumed the tech works." |

**Output.** Your riskiest untested assumption, logged.

### Step 2 · Write hypotheses you can lose (25′)

**Goal.** Turn that assumption into problem hypotheses a stranger could prove wrong this week.

1. **Write 3** in the Experiment Tracker. "[Who] struggles with [problem] when [situation]. Today they [workaround], which costs them [hours or euros]."
2. **Rate the evidence** behind each. Opinion, anecdote, conversation or payment? Only a payment is proof.
3. **Rank them,** riskiest first. Start AI deep research on where your "who" talks.
4. **Log a kill line** in the Kill Criteria Log before you talk to anyone. [The homework](M00_Homework.md#your-kill-line) has one ready.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| "Lab managers in biotech start-ups struggle to track reagent orders when several teams order at once. Today they keep an Excel sheet, which costs them about 4 hours a week." | "Scientists need an AI lab assistant." A solution, a vague who, no cost. |

**Output.** 3 ranked hypotheses, their evidence, a kill line.

### Step 3 · Reach real people, then commit (40′)

**Goal.** Leave with the week already moving.

1. **Find (10′).** Pick named people, not personas. Try people you see every day, product reviewers and your research.
2. **Reach out (15′).** Ask for a short talk about their work. Don't name your problem, and do NOT pitch. Send 5 messages, or call one now. Friends, family and classmates don't count. Their intros do.
3. **Commit (10′).** Copy the bar. "Write 3 problem hypotheses and hold 15 problem conversations with named people, learning what the problem costs each of them today, and come back with 3 people who agreed to a next step. The learning floor is 3 hypotheses and 5 conversations with 1 next step." Pick floor or full bar and a midweek day. 15 a week is 3 a day.
4. **Find teammates (5′).** Tell the room your problem space in one sentence and note who shares it. Teams commit in Module 01. Nobody found? We match you.

| Good | Weak |
|---|---|
| "Hi Anna, I'm talking to lab managers about how their week runs. Could I ask you about yours, 15 minutes?" | "Hi, I'm building an AI tool for lab orders. Would you use it?" |

**Output.** Messages sent, target, pace, possible teammates.

## Arrived empty-handed?

No idea, no technology, no team? You're right on time. Run the autopsy on a frustration you watched someone fight last month, at work or in your lab.
