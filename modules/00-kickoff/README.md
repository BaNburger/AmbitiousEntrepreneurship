# Module 00 — Kickoff — No-Bullshit Entrepreneurship

Updated: 2026-10-19.

> **Core question:** What kills startups — and what will this course demand from you?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, course orientation, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′). The kickoff has no case block in class. The case is read at home as part of this week's homework. Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M00-LG1.** Students can trace a startup's failure to the customer assumption it never tested, and name the riskiest untested assumption in their own starting idea.
2. **M00-LG2.** Students can turn an idea into testable problem hypotheses and say what evidence backs each, calling it a signal until somebody pays.
3. **M00-LG3.** Students can plan a build-measure-learn loop that fits inside one week and start it with the fastest test that reaches real people, before building anything.
4. **M00-LG4.** Students can work to the course contract, setting a weekly target they control, reporting target against actual in a 3-minute read-out, and asking for the help they need.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 01, you have written problem hypotheses and know from named people what those problems cost them today, and those people are your first sales leads.

**This week's bar:** Write 3 problem hypotheses and hold 15 problem conversations with named people, learning what the problem costs each of them today, and come back with 3 people who agreed to a next step. The learning floor is 3 hypotheses and 5 conversations with 1 next step. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).

**Read the case this week.** Read the [Sono Motors case](M00_Case_SonoMotors.md) at home with these three questions in mind.

1. On 8 December 2022 Sono Motors pointed to reservations, pre-orders and its 2019 campaign as proof that people wanted the Sion. Rank this evidence from weakest to strongest. Which of it is a signal, and where does somebody actually pay?
2. Which belief about its customers did the Sion plan rest on, and what in the case tells you whether that belief had been tested by December 2022?
3. You are Hahn and Christians on 8 December 2022. Is a 50-day call to prepay the equivalent of 3,500 Sions the right test of the demand you claim? What result, by what date, should end the Sion?

**The case in one line:** A Munich car start-up holds 21,000 refundable reservations and has to find out who will pay
