# Module 05 — The Solution Space

Updated: 2026-10-19.

> **Core question:** Of all the ways to solve this problem, why this one?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after 4 to 6 hours of preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M05-LG1.** Students can generate several distinct solutions to their tested problem, some without software, converge on one by what people from their segment did with them against a line set in advance, and tell a change of solution (type 1 pivot) from a step back into the problem space (type 2 pivot).
2. **M05-LG2.** Students can write their value proposition for one segment against what that segment uses today, name the one dimension on which it is 10 times better, and back the claim with what they heard or measured, not what they assume.
3. **M05-LG3.** Students can cut their solution to the one feature that delivers its 10x, decide what the interface must show and what may stay manual behind it, and write the result as the smallest offer someone in their segment could pay for, their MVP.
4. **M05-LG4.** Students can build a click dummy of their offer with AI tools in hours, show it to people from their segment in guided demos and behind their landing page, and end each demo with an ask that costs them something, knowing that a click dummy collects signal and only a payment validates.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 06, your team has seen people from your segment try your solution as a click dummy, knows it beats what they use today by far, and holds those who asked for it, your first buyers.

**This week's bar:** Build one click dummy per founder and show the dummies in 15 guided demos per student with people from your segment, at least 7 per dummy, each ending in an ask that costs them something. The learning floor is one click dummy per team and 5 such demos per student. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
