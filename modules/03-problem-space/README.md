# Module 03 — The Problem Space

> **Core question:** Is this a real problem — or just an interesting one?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M02 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Fall in love with the problem, not your solution
2. Jobs-to-be-Done: what customers actually hire your product for
3. The Mom Test: interview questions that produce signal, not polite lies
4. Hair-on-fire segments: find who feels the pain most urgently

**This week's bar:** Pain Map with budget column + JTBD + 5 hair-on-fire interviews. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.pdf).
