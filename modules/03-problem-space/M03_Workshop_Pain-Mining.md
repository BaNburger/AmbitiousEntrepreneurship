# Workshop 03 — Pain Mining

> Module 03 · The Problem Space · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 15m | **Interview Debrief** | Share top findings from 5+ interviews in teams. Empty-handed? You run 2 LIVE calls in this session instead — no spectators. |
| 20m | **Pain Map Build** | Map: who, how often, how bad, current spend, workarounds |
| 20m | **Hair-on-Fire ID** | Which segment has the MOST urgent, painful version? |
| 20m | **JTBD Statement** | Write: When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome] |
| 15m | **Interview Plan v2** | Target 5 more interviews with hair-on-fire segment |

## Discussion Sparks

- “If your customers are not desperate, you don’t have a market.”
- “Show me the receipt—what are they spending on bad alternatives today?”
- “Would someone text you at 2am asking for this? That’s hair-on-fire.”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Pain Map (incl. budget-authority column) + JTBD statement + hair-on-fire segment identified

**Kill criteria:** No one in your interviews described spending money on alternatives
