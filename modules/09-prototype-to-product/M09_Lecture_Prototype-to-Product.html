The Mom Test, for prices

Ask what they pay today, then ask for money

Never ask

"Would you pay €99 a month for this?"

People lie to be polite. Hypotheticals are worthless.

Instead ask

"What are you currently paying to solve this?"

"What did the last solution you tried cost?"

"What's your budget for solving this problem?"

The Mom Test approach to pricing, after Rob Fitzpatrick, The Mom Test (momtestbook.com). Photo: jamesoladujoye on Pixabay.

Objections are data

Answer "too expensive" with "compared to what?"

Usually it's budget, not value. Dig in

1

Anchor high, explain value first

"Agencies save €990 a seat a month. We charge €99."

2

State the price, then stop

"It's €99 a seat a month." Then silence.

3

Handle "too expensive" with curiosity

"Too expensive compared to what?"

4

Close or schedule the next step

Always leave with a commitment.

Never this
// In your code. BAD!
