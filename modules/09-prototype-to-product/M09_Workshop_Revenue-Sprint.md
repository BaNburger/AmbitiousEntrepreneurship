# Workshop 09 — Revenue Sprint

> Module 09 · From Prototype to Product · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 10m | **Revenue Check-In** | Who has money? How much? Public leaderboard. |
| 20m | **Feedback Synthesis** | Compile ALL customer feedback into themes |
| 20m | **Impact/Effort Matrix** | Pick top 3 improvements by impact vs effort |
| 25m | **Build #1 NOW** | Start building the highest-impact improvement in class |
| 15m | **Revenue Targets** | Set 2-week revenue targets. Write them on the wall. |

## Discussion Sparks

- “Nothing motivates like seeing another team make money while you’re iterating.”
- “What’s the #1 thing stopping your next customer from paying?”
- “If you had to 10x revenue in 2 weeks, what would you do differently?”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

#1 improvement started + 2-week revenue target committed + payment link live

**Kill criteria:** You have zero revenue and no concrete plan to change that
