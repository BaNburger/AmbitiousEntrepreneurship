# Module 09 — From Prototype to Product

Updated: 2026-10-30.

> **Core question:** How do you turn users into revenue?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M09-LG1.** Students can choose how their venture earns money, the monetization model and the unit a customer pays for, set the price from the value the product creates and what customers pay today instead of from cost, and test it as a hypothesis, a price and a line committed before the asks, using stated willingness to pay, such as Van Westendorp's four questions, only to pick the price they test.
2. **M09-LG2.** Students can make paying work without a founder in the sale, map the steps from activated user to paying customer, put the ask into the product where users reach the outcome, cut every step between yes and paid, let follow-ups run without them, and turn their first sales into one repeatable offer, the same offer bought again without custom scope.
3. **M09-LG3.** Students can take their product from prototype to production by directing a coding agent, deploying every change from version control with keys kept out of the code, connecting a payment provider's checkout whose webhook records each payment in the product, handing one recurring task to an AI agent they tested on their own cases, and leaving the app builder for their own hosting only when the builder becomes the constraint.
4. **M09-LG4.** Students can set up what real revenue needs in Germany without drowning in it, a team bank account and payment account instead of private ones, numbered invoices with the required fields, the small-business VAT rule, receipts kept from the first sale, and clarity on who owns the code, and can tell when the default GbR's personal liability calls for a UG or GmbH and an hour with a lawyer.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 10, your team sells one offer at a price you tested, customers pay for it inside your live product without a founder in the sale, and the same offer sells again without custom work.

**This week's bar:** Run your price test, let customers pay inside your live product without a founder in the sale, and reach €1,000 cumulative revenue from outside your social graph, with the same offer bought by more than one customer. The learning floor is €500 cumulative, with at least one payment made inside your product. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).

**The arc:** You arrive with your first euros, and Modules 09 and 10 turn them into a repeatable engine. €10k by Demo Day is the ambition, €100k the road after.
