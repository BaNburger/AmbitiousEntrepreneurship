# Module 12 — Fundraising & Demo Day

> **Core question:** Can you tell the story — and show the money?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M11 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. The power law: how venture capital actually decides
2. When (not) to raise: default alive vs. default dead
3. Pitch structure that works: the Sequoia format
4. Demo day: proof beats promises — show the revenue

**Revenue bar this week:** pass €1,000 verified (Demo Day bar) · stretch the €10,000 club — outside your social graph, verified. Full rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
