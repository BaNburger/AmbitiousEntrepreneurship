# Workshop 04 — Landing Page Sprint

> Module 04 · Validating Problems · 90 minutes

**Pre-work (before you arrive):** builder account created (Lovable/v0/Framer) · analytics account ready · your 3 sharpest interview quotes copied out · €50 ad budget source named (team cash or course budget — clarify in check-in).

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 5m | **Examples** | 3 great validation landing pages dissected |
| 40m | **Build Sprint** | Copy FIRST (headline = your sharpest verbatim quote), then build: pain, promise, CTA, email capture |
| 15m | **Copywriting** | Headline from customer language, CTA, social proof |
| 15m | **Analytics + Ads** | Set up tracking, plan €50 ad campaign |
| 15m | **Deploy + Share** | Go live, share URL, peer review each other’s pages |

## Discussion Sparks

- “Your headline should be something a customer SAID, not something you THINK.”
- “If you can’t explain it in 6 words on a landing page, it’s too complicated.”
- “In 2026 there’s zero excuse for not having a page live by end of class.”
