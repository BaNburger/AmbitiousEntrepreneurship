# Module 02 — Finding Ideas

> **Core question:** Where do startup ideas actually come from?

**Session flow:** Intro & check-in (5′) → Recap M01 + homework debrief (10′) → Lecture: the ideas (60′) → Case discussion (30′) → Workshop (90′) → Wrap-up & homework (10′)

## The Ideas

1. Good ideas are noticed, not brainstormed — live in the future, find what's missing
2. Diverge before you converge: volume beats precision (Crazy 8s)
3. DVF — Desirability, Viability, Feasibility as your first filter
4. Founder-problem fit: why YOU, why THIS, why NOW

**This week's bar:** one tested idea + 5 recorded Mom Test interviews. Anti-fake rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
