# Workshop 02 — Idea Stress Test

> Module 02 · Finding Ideas · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 8m | **Crazy 8s Round 1** | 8 ideas in 8 minutes on paper. No filtering. |
| 8m | **Crazy 8s Round 2** | 8 more with constraints: AI-powered, <€500 to build |
| 15m | **DVF Scoring** | Rate each on Desirability, Viability, Feasibility (1-5) |
| 25m | **Peer Attack** | Other teams try to KILL your top 3 ideas |
| 20m | **Survivor Selection** | Narrow to #1 + write Mom Test interview script |
| 14m | **Interview Planning** | Schedule 5 interviews for this week |

## Peer Attack pairing: teams pair A↔B for round one, rotate two seats left for round two — every team attacks two different teams and defends twice. Attacker's job: find the assumption that kills it. Defender's job: answer with evidence or concede.

## Discussion Sparks

- “Which idea would you quit university for tomorrow?”
- “Could a smart intern with Google solve this? If yes, it’s not a startup.”
- “What’s the laziest possible version of this that still delivers value?”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Top 1 validated idea + Mom Test interview script ready

**Kill criteria:** Nobody in the room would pay for your idea—not even hypothetically
