# Workshop 06 — Show & Build

> Module 06 · Prototyping with AI · 90 minutes

## The 90 Minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 30m | **Demo Reviews** | TWO PARALLEL PODS · 3 min per team + 2 min brutal feedback — the math works for 12 teams |
| 35m | **Live Build** | Instructor codes a feature live; teams build alongside |
| 15m | **Cross-Testing** | Try each other’s prototypes, report bugs + reactions |
| 15m | **User Plan** | Name 10 specific non-friends to test this week |

## Discussion Sparks

- “If the demo takes more than 60 seconds to explain, the product isn’t clear.”
- “Show me, don’t tell me. A working demo beats 50 slides.”
- “What broke? What surprised you? That’s where the learning is.”

## Must Produce — Today’s Deliverable

Deployed prototype + feedback log + 10-person outreach list

**Kill criteria:** Your prototype is a Figma mockup, not a working product
