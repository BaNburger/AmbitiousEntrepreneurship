# Module 08 — Finding First Users

Updated: 2026-10-30.

> **Core question:** Who are your first ten customers — by name?

**Session flow:** Input session (about 90′, with check-in, recap and read-out, lecture, wrap-up and homework) · Workshop (90′) · Case discussion (about 90′, after preparation at home). Each block can run on its own.

## Learning goals

1. **M08-LG1.** Students can do things that don't scale on purpose, recruit each first user by hand, turn a yes into use on the spot instead of sending a link, and deliver by hand what the product cannot do yet, a concierge offer included.
2. **M08-LG2.** Students can run founder-led sales, list named prospects who feel the pain, can pay and can be reached, starting from the people their own records hold, and reach them with outreach in the prospect's own words that asks for one next step, followed up until a clear yes or no, with every thread tracked so none drops.
3. **M08-LG3.** Students can map their funnel from stranger to activated user, a user who reached the outcome, count each step per channel, find where most people drop, and double down on the channel that brings activated users from their segment rather than impressions.
4. **M08-LG4.** Students can ask for money early, in the conversation, at the price their offer carries, take the payment through a payment link or a numbered invoice, log every objection word for word, and count only payments from outside their social graph, knowing that any payment, however small, separates a customer from a fan.

**This week's goal.** By the start of Module 09, your team has asked named people from your segment to pay, holds your first customers from outside your social graph, and knows which channel and which ask bring the next ones.

**This week's bar:** Ask named people from your segment to pay for your offer at its price, and win 3 paying users from outside your social graph, shown in your payment dashboard or on a numbered invoice. The learning floor is your first € from outside your social graph. Rules: [Grading & Revenue Ladder](../../resources/GRADING_RUBRIC.md).
