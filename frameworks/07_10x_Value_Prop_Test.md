# The 10x Value Proposition Test
*If you're not 10x better on at least one dimension, you're a feature, not a company.*

---

## The Thiel Question

Peter Thiel asks every startup: "Can you make something 10x better than what exists?"

Not 10% better. Not twice as good. 10x. Because switching costs are real — people won't abandon their current solution for something marginally better. They need a reason so overwhelming that the pain of switching is dwarfed by the value of the new thing.

---

## The Test (Score Yourself Honestly)

### Gate 1: Can you state it in under 10 words?

Write your value proposition here: ___________________________

If you need more than 10 words, you don't understand it well enough yet. Iterate until you can say it in a sentence a child would understand.

Examples that pass:
- "Accept payments in 7 lines of code" (Stripe)
- "Stay in someone's home instead of a hotel" (Airbnb)
- "All your work tools in one app" (Notion)
- "Code faster with AI pair programming" (Cursor)

Examples that fail:
- "An AI-powered platform leveraging machine learning to optimize..."
- "A comprehensive solution for enterprise workflow management..."

### Gate 2: What dimension are you 10x on?

Pick ONE. You can only be 10x on one dimension. Trying to be 10x on everything means you're 1x on everything.

| Dimension | 10x means... | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Speed** | Task that took hours now takes seconds | Cursor (coding), Midjourney (design) |
| **Cost** | 90% cheaper than alternatives | Canva vs. hiring a designer |
| **Access** | Available to people who were locked out before | Stripe (payments for indie devs), Airbnb (affordable travel) |
| **Quality** | Dramatically better output | GPT-4 vs. template-based chatbots |
| **Simplicity** | 90% less complexity to get the same result | Stripe's 7 lines vs. competitors' 7 API calls |
| **Scale** | Handles 10x more volume/users/data | Cloud computing vs. on-premise servers |

Your 10x dimension: ___________________________
Your proof: ___________________________

**If you can't fill in the proof line with a specific, measurable claim, your 10x is imaginary.**

### Gate 3: The Switch Test

> "Would a user abandon their current solution — with all the switching costs, habits, and inertia — to use yours?"

To pass, you need ALL of these:
- [ ] You can name the specific thing they're switching FROM
- [ ] You can articulate why switching is worth the pain
- [ ] At least 3 out of 5 interviewees said "yes, I would switch" after SEEING (not hearing about) your product
- [ ] At least 1 person has actually switched (actions > words)

### Gate 4: The 60-Second Demo

Can you demonstrate the 10x value in under 60 seconds? Not explain it — SHOW it.

Set a timer. Show someone your product. If they don't say "whoa" or "wait, how did you do that?" or "can I use this right now?" within 60 seconds, your 10x isn't visible enough.

**This is the Karpathy rule:** If the value requires a tutorial, onboarding flow, or explanation, it's not 10x. 10x value is self-evident.

---

## Scoring

| Gates Passed | Verdict |
|-------------|---------|
| 4/4 | **Ship it.** You have a 10x value prop. Now execute. |
| 3/4 | **Almost.** Identify which gate you failed and fix ONLY that. |
| 2/4 | **Pivot the solution.** The problem might be right but your approach isn't 10x. |
| 0-1/4 | **Pivot the problem.** You're solving something that doesn't need a 10x solution, or doesn't need solving at all. |

---

## The AI-Specific 10x Check

If you're building an AI product, add this bonus gate:

### Gate 5: The "Smart Intern" Test (Karpathy)

> "Would a smart intern with access to Google accomplish the same thing in the time your user is willing to wait?"

- If the task takes 5 minutes for an intern → AI needs to do it in 5 seconds (100x speed) OR do it at 10x quality.
- If the task takes 1 hour for an intern → AI can take 5 minutes and still be 10x.
- If the task takes 1 week for an intern → You might have something, even if the AI takes a full day.

The key variable is **what the user's time is worth** and **how often they face this task.**

A lawyer billing €400/hour who faces a 2-hour research task 3x per week? AI that does it in 10 minutes at 80% accuracy = massive value.

A student who formats a resume once per year? AI that does it slightly faster = not a company.

---

## Run This Test Every Two Weeks

Your 10x can degrade. Competitors catch up. Customers' expectations shift. Base models improve and eat your moat. Re-run this test every time you feel comfortable. Comfort is a warning sign.
